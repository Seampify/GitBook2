# Messaging Limits for Click-to-WhatsApp Ads (CTWA)

Meta updated its messaging and pricing rules for Click-to-WhatsApp Ads (CTWA) and Facebook Page Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons, effective **28 September 2026**.

With this update, the free messaging window has been extended from 72 hours to **up to 7 days**, giving businesses more time to engage with customers without incurring Meta messaging charges.

Below is a guide on how these changes affect businesses using Seampify.

***

## 1. What Is CTWA?

Click-to-WhatsApp Ads (CTWA) are advertisements on Facebook or Instagram that direct customers to your business's WhatsApp number.

When a customer clicks the ad and sends a WhatsApp message, your business can respond to enquiries, provide assistance, and follow up with potential customers directly through Seampify.

***

## **2. What Has Changed?**

Previously, Meta provided a **72-hour free messaging window** for eligible conversations initiated through CTWA ads.

Under the updated rules:

* The free messaging window has been extended from **72 hours to up to 7 days**.
* Businesses must respond to the customer's initial message within **24 hours** to activate the free messaging window.
* The 7-day window begins when the business sends its first response.
* During this period, eligible Marketing, Utility, Authentication, and Service messages are free of Meta messaging charges.
* The standard **24-hour customer service window** still applies to free-form messages.

This gives businesses more time to follow up with potential customers and convert enquiries into sales.

***

## **3. Important Notes**

**24-Hour Customer Service Window vs. 7-Day Free Messaging Window**

These two messaging windows serve different purposes.

#### **i. 24-Hour Customer Service Window**

* Opens when a customer sends a message.
* Allows businesses to send free-form messages, such as text, images, and interactive messages.
* Refreshes whenever the customer sends a new message.
* Once the 24-hour window closes, businesses must use approved WhatsApp message templates to continue messaging.

#### **ii. 7-Day Free Messaging Window**

* Applies to eligible conversations initiated through CTWA ads or supported Facebook Page CTA buttons.
* Activates when the business replies within 24 hours of the customer's initial message.
* Begins when the business sends its first response and can remain open for up to 7 days.
* Eligible messages sent within this period are free of Meta messaging charges.
* If the 24-hour customer service window has closed, businesses can continue sending approved message templates within the 7-day free messaging window.

**Example:**

A customer clicks your Facebook ad and sends a WhatsApp message on **Monday at 10:00 AM**.

Your business replies at **10:05 AM**.

* **Customer Service Window:** Remains open until Tuesday at 10:00 AM, unless the customer sends another message.
* **Free Messaging Window:** Can remain open until the following Monday at 10:05 AM.
* **After Tuesday at 10:00 AM:** Approved WhatsApp message templates are required unless the customer sends another message.
* **After the 7-Day Window Expires:** Standard WhatsApp messaging charges apply.

#### **iii. Messaging Charges After 7 Days**

Once the free messaging window expires, eligible messages will no longer receive the CTWA free messaging benefit.

Messaging charges will follow Meta's current per-message pricing model, depending on the message category and recipient country.

For more information, refer to [Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform Pricing](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/platform-pricing/).

#### **iv. CTWA Ads Are Not Free**

Although eligible WhatsApp messages can be free for up to 7 days, the advertisement itself is not free.

Businesses will still incur advertising costs when running CTWA campaigns through Meta Ads Manager.

#### **v. Eligibility Requirements**

To qualify for the free messaging window:

* The customer must initiate the conversation through an eligible CTWA ad or supported Facebook Page CTA button.
* The customer must use WhatsApp on a supported Android or iOS device.
* The business must respond within 24 hours of the customer's initial message.

Conversations initiated through ordinary WhatsApp links or unsupported entry points may not qualify for the free messaging benefit.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** The free messaging window does not override Meta's messaging limits or opt-in requirements. Businesses must continue following Meta's applicable messaging policies.
{% endhint %}

## **4. How to Leverage This Update in Seampify?**

#### i. Maximise Customer Engagement

With up to 7 days of free messaging, businesses have more time to respond to customer enquiries and nurture potential leads.

Ensure your team responds promptly to activate the free messaging window.

#### ii. Optimise Ad Spend

Make full use of the free messaging period to engage with customers who have shown interest in your advertisements.

This can help reduce WhatsApp messaging costs while improving lead conversion opportunities.

#### iii. Automate Responses

Using Seampify's chatbot and automation features, businesses can automatically respond when customers initiate conversations through CTWA ads.

**Important:** If the 24-hour customer service window has closed, only approved WhatsApp message templates can be sent, even when the 7-day free messaging window remains active.

#### iv. Create a 7-Day Drip Campaign

Businesses can create a structured follow-up sequence using Seampify to guide customers from their initial enquiry to conversion.

**For example:**

| Timeline  | Suggested Follow-Up                                                                                            |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Day 1** | Send a welcome message and confirm the customer's interest.                                                    |
| **Day 2** | Share product information, services, or frequently asked questions.                                            |
| **Day 3** | Follow up on the customer's enquiry and offer assistance.                                                      |
| **Day 4** | Share testimonials, product benefits, or success stories.                                                      |
| **Day 5** | Provide a personalised recommendation or relevant promotion.                                                   |
| **Day 6** | Encourage the customer to take action, such as booking an appointment or speaking with a sales representative. |
| **Day 7** | Send a final follow-up before the free messaging window expires.                                               |

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** Any follow-up sent outside the 24-hour customer service window must use an approved WhatsApp message template. Schedule the final follow-up before the actual 7-day expiry time. All follow-ups must comply with Meta's messaging and opt-in policies.
{% endhint %}

With Meta extending the CTWA free messaging window from 72 hours to **up to 7 days**, businesses using Seampify have more opportunities to engage with customers and manage follow-ups efficiently.

By responding promptly, using automated messaging, and planning follow-up campaigns effectively, businesses can maximise the benefits of CTWA while reducing messaging costs.
