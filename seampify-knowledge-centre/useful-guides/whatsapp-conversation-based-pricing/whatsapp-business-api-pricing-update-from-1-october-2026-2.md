# Service Message

Service messages are regular, non-template replies sent by a business within an active **24-hour customer service window**.

These include messages sent manually through Seampify Inbox or automatically through supported chatbots and automations.

***

## 1. How Are Service Messages Charged?

Effective **1 October 2026**, Meta introduced per-message charges for Service messages.

Each WhatsApp business phone number receives **1,000 free delivered Service messages per month**.

* The first 1,000 Service messages are free.
* From the 1,001st delivered message, Meta's Service message rate applies.
* The allowance resets every month.
* Unused free messages do not carry forward.

The free allowance applies individually to each business phone number, not to the entire WhatsApp Business Account.

***

## 2. Service Message Rates for Singapore and Malaysia

| Recipient Country | Rate per Message |
| ----------------- | ---------------- |
| Singapore (+65)   | S$0.0205         |
| Malaysia (+60)    | RM0.0564         |

These rates apply after the free monthly allowance has been exhausted.

#### Example: Service Message Charges

A business sends 2,000 Service messages to Malaysian customers in one month.

| Description                 | Messages |
| --------------------------- | -------- |
| Total Service Messages      | 2,000    |
| Free Monthly Allowance      | 1,000    |
| Chargeable Service Messages | 1,000    |

**Estimated Meta charges:**

1,000 × RM0.0564 = **RM56.40**

This example assumes one WhatsApp business phone number and no additional exemptions.

\[Flowchart here: Customer sends a message → 24-hour window opens → Business sends Service messages → First 1,000 delivered messages each month are free → Additional messages are charged.]

#### Important Things to Know

* Incoming customer messages remain free.
* Each delivered Service message is counted separately, even within the same conversation.
* Service messages can only be sent during an active 24-hour customer service window.
* A valid payment method or applicable billing arrangement may be required to continue sending chargeable messages after the free allowance is used.

For more information, refer to [Meta's Official WhatsApp Business Platform Pricing](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/platform-pricing/).
