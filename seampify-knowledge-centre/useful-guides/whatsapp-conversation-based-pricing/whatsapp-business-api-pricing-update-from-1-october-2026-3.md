# Calling

WhatsApp Business Calling allows businesses and customers to communicate through voice calls on the WhatsApp Business Platform.

Meta's calling charges are separate from WhatsApp messaging charges.

***

## 1. Types of WhatsApp Calls

There are two types of WhatsApp Business calls:

1. **User-Initiated Calls:** When a customer calls your business through WhatsApp.
2. **Business-Initiated Calls:** When your business calls a customer through WhatsApp, subject to the required permissions.

***

## 2. How Are WhatsApp Calls Charged?

User-initiated calls are free of Meta's calling connectivity charges, while business-initiated calls are charged based on call duration, the customer's country code, and applicable volume tiers.

#### Calling Rates for Singapore and Malaysia

| Recipient Country | User-Initiated Calls | Business-Initiated Calls |
| ----------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Singapore (+65)   | Free                 | US$0.0114/min            |
| Malaysia (+60)    | Free                 | US$0.0108/min            |

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** Rates are shown in USD per minute and exclude applicable taxes and additional platform fees.
{% endhint %}
