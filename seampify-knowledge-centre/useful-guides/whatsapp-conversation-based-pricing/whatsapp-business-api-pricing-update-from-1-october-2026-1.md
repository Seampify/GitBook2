# Template Message

WhatsApp message templates are pre-approved messages that businesses can send to customers through the WhatsApp Business Platform, including outside the 24-hour customer service window.

Meta charges for each successfully delivered template message based on its category and the recipient's country.

***

## 1. Types of Template Messages

There are three categories of WhatsApp message templates:

1. **Marketing:** Promotional messages, product announcements, special offers, and customer engagement campaigns.
2. **Utility:** Transactional updates, including order confirmations, delivery notifications, and appointment reminders.
3. **Authentication:** One-time passwords (OTPs) used for account verification and security.

***

## 2. How Are Template Messages Charged?

Meta uses a per-message pricing model. Each delivered template message is charged according to its category and the recipient's country.

Since **1 October 2026**, Utility templates sent within the 24-hour customer service window are also chargeable. Previously, these messages were free within the window.

Marketing and Authentication templates remain chargeable according to Meta's applicable rates.

***

## 3. Template Message Rates for Singapore and Malaysia

Rates are per delivered message and exclude applicable taxes. Volume discounts or special Authentication rates may apply to eligible accounts.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** Meta determines messaging charges based on the recipient's country code, not your business location. Eligible messages sent during a free-entry-point window may be exempt from standard charges.
{% endhint %}

For the latest rates, refer to [Meta's Official WhatsApp Business Platform Pricing](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/platform-pricing/).
