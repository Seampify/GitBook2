# Why Can’t I Upload My Contacts?

## 1. **Check File Format**

* **Supported Format:** Ensure your contact file is in CSV (`.csv`) format.
* **Action:** Convert your contact file to CSV format if necessary.
* **Tip:** Use the Seampify sample file template to ensure your file follows the correct structure.

***

## **2. Ensure Proper Data Formatting**

* **Required Columns:** Ensure your file includes the **Phone Number** column (mandatory) and **Name** column (recommended).
* **Invalid Entries:** Check that your file does not contain invalid or incorrectly formatted data.
*   **Phone Number Format:** Ensure phone numbers are entered in the correct format, preferably with the country code and without spaces or special characters.

    **Examples:**

    * Malaysia: `60123456789`
    * Singapore: `6591234567`
* **Action:** Correct any formatting errors and re-upload your file.
