# Broadcast Messaging Limit

## 1. Understanding Messaging Limit

Messaging limits determine the maximum number of unique customers your business can contact using approved [message templates](https://seampify.gitbook.io/seampify/useful-guides/conversation-through-whatsapp-cloud-api/understanding-whatsapp-message-template) outside the 24-hour customer service window, within a rolling 24-hour period.

Meta applies messaging limits at the **business portfolio level**, meaning all WhatsApp business phone numbers within the same portfolio share one messaging limit.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (42).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Messaging Limit Overview</p></figcaption></figure>

#### Messaging Limit Tiers

* **Starting Limit:** 250 unique customers within a rolling 24-hour period.
* **Tier 1:** 2,000 unique customers.
* **Tier 2:** 10,000 unique customers.
* **Tier 3:** 100,000 unique customers.
* **Tier 4:** Unlimited unique customers.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Tip:** Messaging limits do not apply to replies sent within an active 24-hour customer service window. However, applicable Meta messaging charges may still apply.
{% endhint %}

## 2. Increasing Messaging Limits <a href="#increasing_messaging_limits" id="increasing_messaging_limits"></a>

New business portfolios generally start with a limit of 250 unique customers, which can increase as your business meets Meta's requirements.

To increase your limit from **250 to 2,000**, you can complete an eligible scaling path, such as:

* Complete Meta Business Verification.
* Send approved, high-quality message templates to 2,000 unique customers within a rolling 30-day period.

Once your portfolio reaches Tier 1 (2,000), Meta may automatically increase your messaging limit if:

* Your business phone numbers and message templates maintain a **Medium or High** quality rating.
* Your portfolio has reached at least **50% of its current messaging limit** within the past seven days.

If both conditions are met, Meta may increase your limit to the next tier within approximately six hours.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important:** Messaging limit increases are subject to Meta's review of your business's messaging activity, quality, and eligibility. Meeting the volume requirement does not guarantee an upgrade.
{% endhint %}

#### Example 1: Increasing from 2K to 10K in 2 Days

Suppose your business has a messaging limit of 2,000 unique customers.

|                                             | Day 1   | Day 2 | Day 3 | Day 4 |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| New unique customers messaged               | 1,000   | 0     | —     | —     |
| Unique customers reached in the past 7 days | 1,000\* | 1,000 | —     | —     |
| Messaging limit                             | 2K      | 10K   | —     | —     |

_If the 1,000th unique customer is reached at 1:00 PM on Day 1, your messaging limit may increase within approximately six hours, provided Meta's quality and eligibility requirements are met. The increase could therefore occur on Day 1 or become visible by Day 2._

#### Example 2: Increasing from 2K to 10K Over 4 Days

In this example, your business gradually reaches the 50% usage threshold.

|                                             | Day 1 | Day 2 | Day 3 | Day 4    |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----- | ----- | ----- | -------- |
| New unique customers messaged               | 200   | 300   | 300   | 200      |
| Unique customers reached in the past 7 days | 200   | 500   | 800   | 1,000\*  |
| Messaging limit                             | 2K    | 2K    | 2K    | 2K → 10K |

_If the 1,000th unique customer is reached at 3:00 PM on Day 4, your messaging limit may increase to 10K at approximately 9:00 PM on the same day, provided Meta's requirements are met._

**Note:** Both examples are illustrative. Actual upgrades depend on Meta's evaluation and are not guaranteed at a specific time.

## 3. Checking Your Limit <a href="#checking-your-limit" id="checking-your-limit"></a>

You can check your current messaging limit through [WhatsApp Manager](https://business.facebook.com/wa/manage/home/).

#### Step 1: Open WhatsApp Manager

1. Log in to [Meta Business Suite](https://business.facebook.com/).
2. Navigate to **Settings > Accounts > WhatsApp Accounts**.
3. Select your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA).
4. Click **WhatsApp Manager**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (55).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Step 2: Check Your Messaging Limit

In WhatsApp Manager, navigate to **Account Tools > Messaging Limits**.

Here, you can view your business portfolio's current messaging limit and the requirements for increasing it.

The limit is shared across all WhatsApp business phone numbers within the same business portfolio.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (44).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Step 3: Review Your Phone Number Quality

To check your phone number's quality rating:

1. In WhatsApp Manager, open **Account Tools > Phone Numbers**.
2. Select the relevant phone number.
3. Review its connection status and quality rating.

Maintaining a good quality rating is important for messaging-limit increases.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (43).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** Meta may update the WhatsApp Manager interface. Some navigation labels may differ depending on your account.
{% endhint %}
