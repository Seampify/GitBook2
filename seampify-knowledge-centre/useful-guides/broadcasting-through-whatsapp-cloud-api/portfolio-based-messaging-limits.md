# Portfolio-Based Messaging Limits

Since **7 October 2025**, Meta has applied WhatsApp messaging limits at the **business portfolio level**, rather than assigning separate limits to individual phone numbers.

All WhatsApp business phone numbers within the same portfolio now share one messaging limit.

Existing business portfolios inherited the highest messaging limit previously assigned to any phone number within the portfolio.

***

## 1. How Does This Work?

* **Shared Limits:** All WhatsApp business phone numbers within the same portfolio share one messaging limit.
*   **Highest Limit Inherited:** Existing portfolios inherited the highest messaging limit among their connected phone numbers.

    _Example: If Phone Number A previously had a limit of 100K and Phone Number B had 10K, the portfolio inherited a shared limit of 100K._

These changes provide several benefits:

* **Faster Limit Increases:** Eligible portfolios can receive automatic messaging limit upgrades within approximately six hours.
* **Greater Flexibility:** Newly registered phone numbers share the portfolio's existing messaging limit.
* **Simplified Management:** Businesses can manage multiple WhatsApp phone numbers without maintaining separate messaging limits for each number.

***

## 2. Changes <a href="#changes" id="changes"></a>

The following table summarises the differences between the previous phone-number-based system and the portfolio-based system introduced on 7 October 2025.

|                                             | Before 7 October 2025                                               | Since 7 October 2025                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Where are limits applied?                   | Individual business phone numbers                                   | Shared across the business portfolio                                                                             |
| Starting limit for new business portfolios  | Not applicable                                                      | 250 unique customers                                                                                             |
| Starting limit for newly registered numbers | 250                                                                 | Inherits the portfolio's shared limit                                                                            |
| First increased messaging tier              | 1,000                                                               | 2,000                                                                                                            |
| Automatic scaling time                      | Approximately 24 hours                                              | Approximately 6 hours                                                                                            |
| Phone number quality                        | The Flagged quality state could lead to a lower messaging limit     | The former Flagged state was removed; a quality drop does not automatically lower the portfolio limit            |
| Automatic scaling criteria                  | Based on individual phone-number eligibility and messaging activity | Based on messaging quality across the portfolio and usage of at least 50% of the current limit within seven days |

{% hint style="success" %}
**Tip:** Messaging limits determine how many unique customers a business can contact using approved message templates outside the 24-hour customer service window within a rolling 24-hour period.
{% endhint %}

#### Increasing from 250 to 2,000

To increase your messaging limit from 250 to 2,000, complete one of Meta's eligible scaling paths:

* Complete **Meta Business Verification**.
* Have an eligible Business Solution Provider verify your business.
* Send high-quality, approved message templates to at least **2,000 unique customers within a rolling 30-day period**, outside the customer service window.

Meta will evaluate your business's eligibility and messaging quality before determining whether to increase the limit.

#### Increasing Beyond 2,000

Once your business portfolio reaches the 2,000 messaging limit, Meta may automatically increase it to the next tier when:

1. Your business maintains **Medium or High messaging quality** across its connected phone numbers and message templates.
2. Your portfolio has used at least **50% of its current messaging limit within the past seven days**.

When these conditions are met, Meta may increase your messaging limit by one tier within approximately six hours.

**Example:**

If your portfolio has a messaging limit of 2,000 and sends high-quality template messages to at least 1,000 unique customers within seven days, it may qualify for an increase to 10,000.

The same criteria apply when scaling from 10,000 to 100,000 and from 100,000 to Unlimited.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important:** Messaging limit increases are subject to Meta's assessment of your business's messaging quality, activity, and eligibility. Meeting the requirements does not guarantee an upgrade at a specific time.
{% endhint %}
