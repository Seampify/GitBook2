# Blue Tick Verification

## **1. How to Apply for Blue Tick Verification on Your Own (Free Process)**

#### **Requirements for Submitting a Blue Tick Verification Request**

To be eligible for Blue Tick verification, ensure the following criteria are met:

1. **Use an Approved WhatsApp API Business Account** – Your business must be connected to the WhatsApp Business Platform through Seampify.
2. **Enable Two-Step Verification** – Your WhatsApp Business phone number must have two-step verification enabled.
3. **Verify Your Meta Business Portfolio** – Your business portfolio must have completed Meta Business Verification.
4. **Get Your Display Name Approved** – Ensure your WhatsApp Business display name has been approved.
5. **Meet the Minimum Account Age** – Your business must have been registered on the WhatsApp Business Platform for at least 30 days.
6. **Comply with Meta's Policies** – Your business must comply with the [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/).
7. **Demonstrate Notability** – Your business must represent a well-known, frequently searched brand or entity. Independent news articles and reputable third-party media coverage can help demonstrate your brand's public recognition. Self-published or paid promotional articles generally do not establish independent notability.

## **2. Steps to Apply for Blue Tick Verification via Seampify**

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### **Go to Your Meta Business Settings**

Navigate to **Business Settings → WhatsApp Accounts → WhatsApp Manager**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (264).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### **Access the Phone Numbers Section**

Select the WhatsApp business phone number you want to verify.

Then, click the **Settings** icon or open the phone number's settings, depending on your WhatsApp Manager interface.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (265).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### **Submit an Official Business Account Request**

Open the **Profile** tab and locate the **Official Business Account** section.

Next, click **Submit Request** and complete the required information.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (267).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Provide up to **five supporting links** from reputable third-party publications to demonstrate your brand's recognition. Examples include independent news articles and credible media coverage.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (230).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### **Await Meta's Review**

Meta will review your application and notify you of the outcome. Review times may vary.

Ensure all submitted information is accurate and that your supporting publications demonstrate your business's public recognition.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** If the **Submit Request** option is unavailable, check that your business meets the eligibility requirements. You may also contact Meta Support or your solution provider for assistance.
{% endhint %}

***

## **3. Is Blue Tick Verification Guaranteed?**

Blue Tick verification is different from WhatsApp API approval. Being approved for the WhatsApp API **does not guarantee** Official Business Account (OBA) verification.

* Meta grants Blue Tick status to businesses that meet its eligibility and notability requirements.
* The decision to approve or reject a Blue Tick verification request is made solely by **Meta**, not Seampify.

If your application is **rejected**, you can **reapply after 30 days**. During this period, consider strengthening your business's online presence through reputable third-party media coverage.

For further details, refer to Meta's official documentation: [WhatsApp Business Accounts](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/overview/business-accounts#official-business-account).
