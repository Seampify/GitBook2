# Phone Number Quality Rating

Your business phone number's status and quality rating can be viewed in [WhatsApp Manager](https://business.facebook.com/wa/manage/home/) under **Account Tools → Phone Numbers**.

Your business portfolio's messaging limit can be viewed under **Account Tools → Messaging Limits**.

Note that it is normal for phone numbers with high messaging traffic to experience quality-rating changes within short intervals, sometimes even within minutes.

***

## 1. About Your WhatsApp Business Phone Number's Quality Rating

Your quality rating is based on how customers respond to messages received from your business over the past seven days. Recent customer feedback carries more weight in Meta's assessment.

You can find your quality rating under the **Phone Numbers** tab in WhatsApp Manager. The **Quality Rating** column displays the following states:

* 🟢 **Green: High Quality**
* 🟡 **Yellow: Medium Quality**
* 🔴 **Red: Low Quality**

Maintaining a High quality rating can help your business remain eligible for additional messaging features.

When customers block your business, they may select a reason, including:

* No longer needed
* Didn't sign up
* Spam
* Offensive messages
* No reason provided

If your quality rating is Medium or Low, you can hover over the rating in WhatsApp Manager to view available insights into customer block reasons.

These insights can help you identify problematic message templates, understand customer feedback, and reduce the risk of further blocks.

You may also receive notifications through Meta Business Manager when important messaging or account-quality changes occur.

To review your quality rating over the past 30 days, click the relevant icon in the **Settings** column. This displays the lowest quality rating recorded each day, helping you identify possible reasons for changes in your messaging quality.

## 2. Status Changes

Since **7 October 2025**, Meta has applied messaging limits at the **business portfolio level**, rather than assigning separate limits to individual phone numbers.

All WhatsApp business phone numbers within the same portfolio now share one messaging limit.

The previous **Flagged** phone number status and its automatic messaging-limit downgrade mechanism have been discontinued.

A Low quality rating no longer automatically reduces your messaging limit to the previous tier. However, poor messaging quality can affect your eligibility for future messaging-limit increases.

#### Connected

Your WhatsApp business phone number is active and can send outgoing messages within your business portfolio's available messaging limit.

#### Low Quality Rating

When your phone number's quality rating changes to Low, review your recent messages and customer feedback.

Although a Low quality rating does not automatically reduce your portfolio's messaging limit, it may prevent your business from qualifying for an increase.

Repeated policy violations or poor messaging practices may also result in additional restrictions from Meta.

#### Reaching Your Messaging Limit

When your business portfolio reaches its messaging limit, you cannot initiate additional template messages to new customers outside the customer service window until sufficient capacity becomes available within the rolling 24-hour period.

You can still respond to customer-initiated messages within the 24-hour customer service window, subject to Meta's policies.

For more information, refer to [Broadcast Messaging Limit](https://seampify.gitbook.io/seampify/useful-guides/broadcasting-through-whatsapp-cloud-api/broadcast-messaging-limit).

## 3. Improve Your Quality Rating <a href="#improve_your_quality_rating" id="improve_your_quality_rating"></a>

To improve your quality rating, consider the following steps:

* **Check Templates:** Review message templates sent within the past seven days to identify any that may be causing negative customer feedback. Update or stop using problematic templates to improve message quality.
* **Policy Compliance:** Ensure your messages comply with [WhatsApp's Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/). Review your messaging content regularly to avoid policy violations.
* **User Opt-In:** Confirm that customers have agreed to receive the types of messages you send. Sending messages without appropriate consent may result in blocks or reports, negatively affecting your quality rating.
* **Best Practices:** Review your messaging strategy against [WhatsApp's Best Practices Guide](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/message-templates/guidelines). Focus on sending relevant, timely, and valuable messages that improve customer engagement.

By following these practices, you can improve customer satisfaction, reduce negative feedback, and maintain a healthy messaging reputation on WhatsApp.
