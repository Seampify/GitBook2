# What Happens If My Phone Quality Drops?

To maintain a high-quality messaging experience on the WhatsApp Business Platform, businesses must comply with the [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/), [WhatsApp Commerce Policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/commerce-policy/), and [WhatsApp Business Terms of Service](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/business-terms).

Meta monitors customer feedback and messaging activity to identify poor messaging practices and policy violations.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** A drop in your phone number's quality rating does not automatically result in a messaging-limit downgrade. However, low messaging quality may affect your eligibility for future messaging-limit increases.
{% endhint %}

***

## 1. What happens?

If your business violates Meta's policies, Meta may issue a warning explaining the violation and any required corrective actions.

If your business continues to violate WhatsApp's policies, such as sending spam, misclassifying message templates, or sending prohibited content involving adult products, alcohol, tobacco, drugs, gambling, or unsafe supplements, Meta may apply messaging restrictions.

Depending on the severity and frequency of the violations, these restrictions may include:

* **Temporary Messaging Restrictions:** Meta may temporarily restrict your ability to send certain message templates or add new phone numbers.
* **Extended Restrictions:** Repeated violations may result in longer restrictions on messaging and account features.
* **Account Lock:** Your business account may be suspended until the issue is resolved or an eligible appeal is reviewed.
* **Permanent Account Disablement:** Continued or serious policy violations may result in permanent removal from the WhatsApp Business Platform.

In severe cases, including child exploitation, scams, terrorism, or the sale of illegal drugs, Meta may immediately disable a business account without issuing multiple warnings.

Meta may also restrict your business account if it receives excessive negative customer feedback.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important:** Restrictions depend on the type and severity of the violation. Not every quality-rating drop results in a warning or account restriction.

Depending on the violation type and eligibility, you may acknowledge the violation or submit an appeal.
{% endhint %}

***

## 2. How to Check Violations and Appeal Them

If your business account violates a policy, you can review the details in **Meta Business Support Home**.

#### How to Access Account Overview

1. Go to [Meta Business Suite](https://business.facebook.com/) and log in to your account.
2. Click **All Tools** in the left-hand menu and select **Business Support Home**.
3. Click **Account Overview** to review your business account's status.

In Account Overview, you can find information such as:

* A summary of the policy violation and a link to the relevant policy.
* Examples of content that is allowed or prohibited under the policy.
* Active restrictions and possible consequences of continued violations.
* Recommendations and resources to help prevent future violations.
* Available options to request a review.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** The available information and navigation may vary depending on your Meta Business Suite interface.
{% endhint %}

#### How to Appeal a Violation

If your business account is eligible for an appeal, follow these steps:

1. Open **Business Support Home** and select the affected business account.
2. Find the relevant policy violation and click **Request Review**, if available.
3. Complete the review request form and provide any necessary supporting information.
4. Click **Submit** to send your appeal.
5. Monitor your appeal status in Business Support Home.

Meta will review your appeal and notify you of the outcome. Review times may vary depending on the type of violation.

The decision may result in the restriction being maintained or reversed.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Note:** Not all violations are eligible for appeal. The **Request Review** option will only appear when available for your account.
{% endhint %}

***

## 3. How to Avoid Future Violations

To maintain good messaging quality and reduce the risk of restrictions:

* **Follow Meta's Policies:** Review the [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/) and [WhatsApp Commerce Policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/commerce-policy/) regularly.
* **Obtain Customer Consent:** Ensure customers have agreed to receive messages from your business before sending business-initiated communications.
* **Send Relevant Messages:** Avoid spam, excessive messaging, and content that may lead customers to block or report your business.
* **Respond to Warnings Promptly:** Review any policy violations and adjust your messaging practices to prevent further enforcement actions.

For more information about monitoring your messaging quality, refer to [Phone Number Quality Rating](https://seampify.gitbook.io/seampify/useful-guides/whatsapp-api-business-account/phone-number-quality-rating).
