# How to Choose a WhatsApp Business Display Name for Your Business

Your WhatsApp Business display name is the name customers see when interacting with your business. To ensure approval and compliance with Meta's policies, your display name must follow specific guidelines and may undergo a review.

In this guide, we'll explain the best practices for choosing an appropriate WhatsApp Business display name.

***

## 1. Guidelines for WhatsApp Business Display Names

| **1. Compliance with Policies**                           | _Sarah's Sporting Goods Shop_: Follows business naming conventions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | _Sarah's Sporting Goods Shop – Guns Department_: Violates WhatsApp Commerce Policy.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **2. Accurate and Clear Representation of Your Business** | <p><em>Fresh Produce Boston</em>: Includes a location for clarity.<br><br><em>Fresh Produce Customer Service Department</em>: Indicates the department.<br><br><em>Fresh Produce by Global Grocers Inc.</em>: Identifies the parent company.</p>                                                | <p><em>Tom Ford Nike</em>: No established relationship between the brands.<br><br><em>Fashion</em>: Too generic.<br><br><em>New York</em>: Location without business context.</p>                                                                                                                                                        |
| **3. Avoid Misleading or Unauthorised Terms**             | _Minni's Muffins_: Represents the business without misleading terms.                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | <p><em>Fresh Produce Official Account</em>: Implies official verification.<br><br><em>Minni's Muffins WhatsApp</em>: Uses a Meta product name.<br><br><em>20% Off Sale</em>: Promotional language.<br><br><a href="https://minnismuffins.net"><em>https://minnismuffins.net</em></a>: Website URLs are not allowed as display names.</p> |
| **4. Clear Relationship with Your Business**              | _Fruit Snacks by Fresh Produce_: If the relationship is stated on an official website.                                                                                                                                                                                                          | <p><em>Digital Cloud Solutions</em>: If the name cannot be found in external business references.<br><br><em>Juice</em>: Too generic.</p>                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **5. Consistency with External Branding**                 | <p><em>FP Cold Pressed Juices</em>: If the abbreviation is used in established branding.<br><br><em>FPCP Juices</em>: If the shortened name is supported by external branding.<br><br><em>XY Fresh Cold Pressed Juices Service New York</em>: If regional branding is publicly established.</p> | _Random Business Name_: Does not match external branding.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** If your display name includes a personal name, it should clearly represent a business and be supported by external sources. For example, _Tom Ford Chiropractor_ may be acceptable if it is the established business name.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important:** For more detailed requirements, refer to [Meta's WhatsApp Business Display Name Guidelines](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/757569725593362).
{% endhint %}

## **3. Business Phone Number Display Name Verification**

#### **i. Conditions for the Display Name to Appear**

Your business phone number must meet the following conditions:

1. The WhatsApp Business display name must be reviewed and approved by Meta (`name_status: APPROVED`).
2. Your business portfolio must complete **Meta Business Verification** or meet an alternative eligible messaging-limit requirement for display-name visibility.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** Display-name approval and Meta Business Verification are separate processes. Submitting a display name does not automatically mean it has been approved or will be visible in all customer chat interfaces.
{% endhint %}

#### **ii. How to Set a Display Name**

1. Open **WhatsApp Manager**.
2. Navigate to **Account Tools → Phone Numbers**.
3. Select the relevant business phone number.
4. Open the display-name settings and enter your preferred name.
5. Submit the name for Meta's review, if required.

You can also add or edit your business phone number's display name through [WhatsApp Manager](https://business.facebook.com/latest/whatsapp_manager).

You can set your display name when [connecting to WhatsApp Cloud API](https://seampify.gitbook.io/seampify/starting-with-seampify/connecting-to-whatsapp-cloud-api).

## **4. What Happens After Business Verification?**

After completing business verification and meeting Meta's display-name requirements, check your display-name review status in WhatsApp Manager.

✅ **Approved:** Your display-name status is set to `APPROVED`. Your approved name can appear to customers once the applicable visibility requirements are met.

❌ **Rejected:** Your display-name request is declined. Review Meta's guidelines, correct the name, and resubmit it through WhatsApp Manager.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important:** Completing Meta Business Verification does not automatically guarantee display-name approval or an increase in messaging limits. Messaging-limit upgrades depend on Meta's separate eligibility and scaling requirements.
{% endhint %}

For more information, refer to [Broadcast Messaging Limit](https://seampify.gitbook.io/seampify/useful-guides/broadcasting-through-whatsapp-cloud-api/broadcast-messaging-limit).
