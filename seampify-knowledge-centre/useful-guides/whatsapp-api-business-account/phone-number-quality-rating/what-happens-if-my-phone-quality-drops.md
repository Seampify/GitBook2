# How to Apply for a Username

{% hint style="info" %}
**Info:** Only eligible WhatsApp Business Accounts can reserve or create a WhatsApp Business Username. Feature availability may vary depending on Meta's rollout.
{% endhint %}

WhatsApp Business Accounts can reserve or create a unique WhatsApp Business Username, making it easier for customers to find and contact their business.

The application process depends on whether your account uses **WhatsApp Cloud API (WABA)** or **WhatsApp Business App Coexistence**.

***

## 1. How to Create Your Business Username

Follow the instructions below based on your WhatsApp Business Account type.

#### For WABA Accounts

WhatsApp Business Platform users can reserve a username through **Meta Business Suite** or **WhatsApp Manager**.

**Using Meta Business Suite:**

1. Go to [Meta Business Suite](https://business.facebook.com/).
2. Click **Settings** and navigate to **WhatsApp Accounts**.
3. Select your WhatsApp Business Account.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (274).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Select the business phone number you want to create a username for.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (279).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Navigate to the **Username** section and click **Create**.
6. Enter your preferred username or select one of Meta's suggestions.
7. Click **Save**, then **Close**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (283).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Alternatively, using WhatsApp Manager:**

1. Open [WhatsApp Manager](https://business.facebook.com/wa/manage/home/).
2. Select **Phone number** from the left-hand menu and choose your business phone number.
3. Open the **Profile** tab and scroll to **Username**.
4. Click **Create** and enter your preferred username.
5. Click **Save**, then **Close**.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** Changes made through Meta Business Suite or WhatsApp Manager are synchronised across both platforms.
{% endhint %}

#### For Coexistence Accounts

Businesses using WhatsApp Business App Coexistence can manage username reservations through the WhatsApp Business app when the feature is available.

To reserve a username:

1. Open the **WhatsApp Business app** on your mobile device.
2. Open your business profile or account settings.
3. Select **Username** or **Reserve Username**, depending on your app version.
4. Enter your preferred username.
5. Follow the on-screen instructions to save your reservation.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (293).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
**Info:** Username availability and approval are subject to Meta's requirements. If the Username option is unavailable, update your WhatsApp Business app and check again when the feature becomes available.
{% endhint %}

### Eligibility Requirements

Before applying, ensure your WhatsApp Business Account meets Meta's applicable requirements.

#### i. WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)

For businesses using **WhatsApp Cloud API**:

* Your WhatsApp Business Account must be active and eligible for username reservation.
* You must have the necessary permissions to manage the business phone number.
* You must comply with Meta's applicable WhatsApp Business Platform terms and policies.
* You can manage your username through WhatsApp Manager or Meta Business Suite.
* Additional account eligibility requirements may apply depending on Meta's rollout and your business configuration.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important:** Some account configurations may have additional business verification or messaging-limit requirements. Check the eligibility information displayed in your Meta account.
{% endhint %}

#### ii. Coexistence Accounts

For businesses using the **WhatsApp Business app with Coexistence**:

* Your WhatsApp Business app account must be active.
* Your account must be eligible for the Username feature.
* You can reserve or manage your username through the WhatsApp Business app when the option becomes available.

## 5. Business Username Requirements

Before submitting your preferred username, ensure it follows Meta's username guidelines.

#### Username Length

Your username must contain:

* At least **3 characters**.
* No more than **35 characters**.

#### Supported Characters

You can use:

* English letters (`a–z`)
* Numbers (`0–9`)
* Periods (`.`)
* Underscores (`_`)

Your username must contain at least one English letter.

#### Username Restrictions

Your username:

* Cannot start with a period (`.`).
* Cannot end with a period (`.`).
* Cannot contain two consecutive periods (`..`).
* Cannot start with `www`.
* Cannot end with certain restricted domain extensions.
* Cannot consist entirely of numbers.
* Is not case-sensitive.
* Must be unique and comply with Meta's restricted-word and naming policies.

#### Examples

**Valid username formats:**

* `JasperMarket`
* `jasper.market`
* `jasper_market`
* `JasperMarketMY`

**Invalid username formats:**

* `.JasperMarket`: Starts with a period.
* `JasperMarket.`: Ends with a period.
* `Jasper..Market`: Contains consecutive periods.
* `www.JasperMarket`: Starts with a restricted prefix.
* `123456`: Contains only numbers.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** These examples illustrate username formatting. Valid formatting does not guarantee availability or approval.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="success" %}
**Tip:** Choose a username that is easy for customers to recognise and closely represents your business or brand.
{% endhint %}

## 6. What Happens After You Apply?

After submitting your preferred username, Meta will check its availability and whether it meets the applicable requirements.

If your username is successfully reserved, it will be associated with your WhatsApp business phone number.

**Important:** Reserving a username does not necessarily mean it is immediately active. Meta will notify you when your reserved username is ready to use.

Once the feature becomes available for your account and region, customers can use your active username to find and contact your business.

***

## 7. What If My Preferred Username Is Already Taken?

WhatsApp Business Usernames must be unique.

If your preferred username is unavailable, you will need to choose another available username.

**Example:**

If `@JasperMarket` is unavailable, you could try:

* `@JasperMarketMY`
* `@Jasper.Market`
* `@JasperMarketOfficial`

All alternatives are subject to availability and Meta's naming requirements.

If the unavailable username belongs to your existing Facebook or Instagram account, you may be able to claim it by linking the relevant account and verifying ownership.

***

## 9. What If I Cannot See the Username Option?

If you cannot find the Username option in Meta Business Suite, WhatsApp Manager, or the WhatsApp Business app, check the following:

#### i. Check Your Eligibility

Ensure your WhatsApp Business Account meets Meta's current eligibility requirements.

#### ii. Check Business Verification

Confirm whether your account requires Meta Business Verification and complete it if necessary.

#### iii. Check Your Messaging Limit

Review your business portfolio's messaging limit and any additional eligibility requirements displayed in your Meta account.

#### iv. Check Your Account Status

Ensure your WhatsApp Business Account is active and is not restricted because of policy violations.

#### v. Check Feature Availability

WhatsApp Usernames are being introduced gradually.

Even if your account meets the applicable requirements, the feature may not be available in your region immediately.

If you are using the WhatsApp Business app, ensure you have the latest version installed.

#### vi. Important Notes

* **Optional Feature:** WhatsApp Business Usernames are optional.
* **Phone Number Required:** A username does not replace your registered WhatsApp Business phone number.
* **Unique Username:** Each business phone number must have its own unique username.
* **Username Availability:** Your preferred username may already be claimed or reserved.
* **Gradual Rollout:** Meeting the eligibility requirements does not guarantee immediate access.
* **Meta Approval:** Meta may update username requirements, availability, and application procedures as the feature continues to roll out.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions <a href="#frequently-asked-questions" id="frequently-asked-questions"></a>

<details>

<summary>Does Business Verification guarantee that I can create a username?</summary>

No.

Business Verification may be one of the applicable eligibility requirements, but completing it does not automatically guarantee access to the Username feature.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I use any username I want?</summary>

No.

Your preferred username must follow Meta's character and formatting requirements and must be available.

Some usernames may be reserved for their existing owners on Facebook or Instagram.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I change my username later?</summary>

Yes.

You can change your business username through WhatsApp Manager or Meta Business Suite, subject to the options available for your account.

After changing your username, Meta holds your previous username for **60 days**. You can reclaim it during this period, and other businesses cannot claim it while the hold remains active.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Does my Business Username replace my phone number?</summary>

No.

Your WhatsApp Business phone number remains connected to your WhatsApp Business Account.

A Business Username provides an additional way for customers to find and contact your business.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I create a username for every WhatsApp number?</summary>

Yes, eligible WhatsApp business phone numbers can each have a unique username.

However, multiple phone numbers cannot share the same username, even when they belong to the same business portfolio.

</details>
