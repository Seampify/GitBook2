# How to Delete WhatsApp Account on Mobile App

To register an existing phone number on the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API), you may need to delete the WhatsApp account currently associated with that number.

You can use a phone number previously registered on WhatsApp Messenger or the WhatsApp Business app. However, for standard Cloud API registration, the existing WhatsApp account must first be deleted.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important:** Eligible WhatsApp Business app users may be able to connect to Cloud API without deleting their accounts through Meta's WhatsApp Coexistence feature. Contact the Seampify support team to confirm your available onboarding options before proceeding.
{% endhint %}

***

## 1. How to Delete Your WhatsApp Account

To delete a WhatsApp Messenger or WhatsApp Business account from your mobile device, follow these steps:

To delete a WhatsApp Messenger or WhatsApp Business account from your mobile device, follow these steps:

1. Open **WhatsApp** or the **WhatsApp Business app** on your primary mobile device.
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete My Account**. On Android, you may need to tap **More Options (⋮)** to access Settings.
3. Enter your phone number in [full international format](https://faq.whatsapp.com/2138577903196467) and tap **Delete My Account**.
4. Select a reason for deleting your account, if prompted.
5. Tap **Delete My Account** again to confirm.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Tip:** Once your WhatsApp account has been deleted, you can proceed with registering the phone number on the WhatsApp Business Platform when it becomes available. The registration process may take some time to reflect the change.
{% endhint %}

***

## 2. What Happens When You Delete Your Account <a href="#deleting_your_account_will" id="deleting_your_account_will"></a>

When you delete your WhatsApp account, the following changes will occur:

* Your WhatsApp account information and profile will be deleted.
* Your message history will be erased.
* You will be removed from all WhatsApp groups.
* Your WhatsApp chat backups associated with the account will be deleted.
* The QR code associated with your previous WhatsApp account will become invalid.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Important:** Deleting your WhatsApp account is irreversible. WhatsApp cannot restore or transfer chat history, media, or backups from a deleted account.
{% endhint %}

***

## 3. Back Up Your WhatsApp Account

Before deleting your WhatsApp account, make sure you have saved any important conversations and media files you may need.

You can find instructions for backing up your account below:

* **iPhone:** [How to Back Up WhatsApp on iPhone](https://faq.whatsapp.com/902477924463699/)
* **Android:** [How to Back Up WhatsApp on Android](https://faq.whatsapp.com/481135090640375/)

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** A backup does not guarantee that your chat history can be restored or transferred after account deletion.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="success" %}
**Seampify Support:** Seampify may be able to assist with transferring your WhatsApp conversation history to your Seampify account. Contact our support team to confirm the available options and requirements **before deleting your WhatsApp account**.
{% endhint %}

For more information, refer to [WhatsApp's official account deletion guide](https://faq.whatsapp.com/2138577903196467).
