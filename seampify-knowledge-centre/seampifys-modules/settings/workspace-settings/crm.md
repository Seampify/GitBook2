# CRM

## CRM Stage Configuration

The **CRM Settings** section allows businesses to customize their Lead Lifecycle Stages and Deal Pipeline Stages to align with their internal sales processes.

Navigate to **Settings → CRM** to access this configuration.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Leads and Deals creation.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Available Configuration Options

| **Configuration** | **Description**                                                                    |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stage Name        | Define a name for each stage based on your sales process.                          |
| Stage Percentage  | Configure a percentage representing the stage's estimated progress or probability. |
| Stage Color       | Assign a color to visually distinguish each stage.                                 |
| Stage Arrangement | Rearrange stages using the drag handle to reflect your preferred workflow.         |
| Add Stage         | Create additional stages for your lead or deal pipeline.                           |
| Save CRM Settings | Save and apply the CRM configuration.                                              |

### Examples of Customized CRM Stages

Different industries may require different sales processes. Businesses can configure their CRM stages according to their operational requirements.

| **Industry** | **Example Lead Stages**                    | **Example Deal Stages**                          |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Property     | New Enquiry, Interested, Qualified         | Viewing Scheduled, Viewed, Booking, SPA Signing  |
| Automotive   | New Enquiry, Interested, High Potential    | Test Drive, Quotation, Loan Application, Booking |
| Education    | New Enquiry, Follow-up, Qualified          | Consultation, Application, Payment, Enrollment   |
| Retail       | New Enquiry, Interested, Ready to Purchase | Product Selection, Quotation, Payment            |
| B2B Services | New Prospect, Qualified, High Potential    | Meeting Set, Demo, Proposal, Negotiation         |

**Note:** These are suggested configurations and are not predefined stages within Seampify.

## Contacts vs Leads vs Deals

| **Criteria**        | **Contacts**                    | **Leads**                          | **Deals**                         |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Main Purpose        | Customer management             | Sales opportunity tracking         | Revenue opportunity management    |
| Represents          | An individual or business       | A potential sales opportunity      | A qualified sales opportunity     |
| Sales Readiness     | Any level                       | Initial to strong interest         | More established buying interest  |
| Pipeline Stages     | Contact stage, where applicable | Customizable Lead Lifecycle Stages | Customizable Deal Pipeline Stages |
| Stage Configuration | —                               | Settings → CRM                     | Settings → CRM                    |
| Monetary Value      | Not required                    | Estimated opportunity value        | Expected deal value               |
| Example             | Customer requesting information | Customer interested in a product   | Customer reviewing a quotation    |

<summary>Benefits of CRM</summary>

* Centralize customer information in a single database.
* Organize and segment customers using labels, lists, and contact attributes.
* Track potential customers throughout the sales journey.
* Monitor lead qualification and deal progress.
* Improve sales follow-up and team accountability.
* Track potential revenue and expected closing dates.
* Improve customer relationship management and sales efficiency.

**Note:** Available CRM features, contact attributes, and pipeline options may vary depending on your organization's configuration and user permissions.
