# Roles & Permissions

Through **Roles & Permissions** Setting, you can create different roles and allow specific permissions to them. Then, use Roles to create Team members and provide them control as per allowed permissions. This is useful to give team members limited control over the Seampify platform and its options.

## Create a Role

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
At Roles & Permissions window, click the **Create Role** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Roles (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Define a name for the Role and click **Save**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Roles (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Assign Permission

Once you've created a Role, you'd find it in the tab. Then, to assign permissions, simply click the what permission this role can do.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Roles (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Or select them all by click Select All.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Roles (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Editing a Role

To update the permission of the role:

* Choose the role
* Click the permission to enable or disable it.
* Then, click **Save Changes**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Roles (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To edit a Role's Name, click the Menu (three vertical dots) icon, then select **Edit Details**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Roles (6).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

It will allow you to change the name and description. Once edited, click **Save**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Roles (7).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Delete a Role

To delete a Role, click the Menu (three vertical dots) icon, then select Delete.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Roles (8).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Then click the **Confirm** button to finalise deleting.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Roles (9).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
