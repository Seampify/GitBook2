# Teams

Team setting allows you to create teams or department at Seampify. You can provide them with complete control or limited based on their team. Using their account, your team members can perform different tasks on assigned channels based on their [Team](teams.md) and User [Role & Permission](../roles-and-permissions.md).

This streamlines the process of managing Channels connections to the different chat platforms. Enabling you and your team to respond to users/contacts in a timely manner and improve user-client relationships for the Business.

## Creating a Team

{% hint style="info" %}
You need to [create a Role](../roles-and-permissions.md#create-a-role) before creating a team.
{% endhint %}

On the Team window, click **Create Team** button.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/Teams (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

This will open the create team modal with the following fields,

* Name
* Description
* Channels
* Team Members

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/Teams (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Simply add the information, and once you've added all click **Create Team**. As soon as you save the Team member it will appear on the page like the below list.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/Teams (6).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
