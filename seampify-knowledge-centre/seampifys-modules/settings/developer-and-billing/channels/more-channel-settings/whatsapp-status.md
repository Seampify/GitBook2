# WhatsApp Status

The **Status** section allows you to monitor the current condition of your connected WhatsApp number.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/wasap status.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can use **Refresh Status** to check for updated information about your WhatsApp connection, quality rating, verification, and messaging capacity.

Simply hover over the info mark **`ⓘ`** to view the description and explanation of each metric.
