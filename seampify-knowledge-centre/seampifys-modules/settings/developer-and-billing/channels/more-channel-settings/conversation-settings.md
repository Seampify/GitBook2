# Conversation Settings

The **Conversation** section allows you to configure conversational components displayed in your WhatsApp chat.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/conv (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

It contains two features:

* Ice Breakers
* Commands

## Ice Breakers

**Ice Breakers** are conversation starters that your customers can see when they first message your business.

They help customers initiate conversations by selecting a suggested topic.

### Creating an Ice Breaker

1. Click **+ Add icebreaker**.
2. Enter the required information.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/conv (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click **Add icebreaker**.

From your customer's side, it will look like this. They can simply tap on the icebreaker to get started.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/convo (4).jpeg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Commands

**Commands** allow you to configure slash shortcuts that customers can use within your WhatsApp chat.

Examples include `/track` and `/help`

### Creating a Command

1. Select the **Commands** tab.
2. Click **+ Add command**.
3. Configure your command using the available fields.
4. Then, click

Commands provide customers with shortcuts to specific conversation actions or topics.
