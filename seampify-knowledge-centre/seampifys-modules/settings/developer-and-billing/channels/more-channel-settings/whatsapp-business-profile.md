## QR Code Studio

You will have a generic QR code linked to your WhatsApp number (wa.me/your-number). You can download the QR code or simply copy the link and share it with your customers.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/channel pro (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Alternatively, you can customise your own QR code using **QR Code Studio**.

The **QR Code Studio** allows you to create WhatsApp QR codes that open a conversation with your business number using a prefilled message.

The customised QR code can also be used as a trigger for your automation. Simply set a pre-filled message for the QR code, and when customers scan it and send the pre-filled message to your WhatsApp, the corresponding automation will be triggered automatically.

### Creating a QR Code

1. Navigate to your WhatsApp Business Profile.
2. Locate the **QR Code Studio** section.
3. Click **+ Create QR Code**.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/channel pro (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Enter the QR code name and prefilled message.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/channel pro (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Review the destination WhatsApp number.

Fill these field to customize your qr code.

* Name - Enter a name to identify your QR code.
* Prefilled Message - Enter the message that will appear when your customer scans the QR code. Maximum 300 characters.

6. Click **Create**.

### Sharing Your QR Code

After creating your QR code, you can:

* **Copy Link:** Copy the WhatsApp click-to-chat URL.
* **Download QR:** Download the QR code image for sharing or printing.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/channel pro (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important:** QR codes created through this studio are retained on the current page only. There is currently no API for saving these QR codes, so download the QR codes you need before leaving the page.

Scanning a QR code opens WhatsApp with the configured message already entered. Your customer must still press **Send** to submit the message.
{% endhint %}
