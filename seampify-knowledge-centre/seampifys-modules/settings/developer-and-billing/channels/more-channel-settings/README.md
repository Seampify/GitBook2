# More Channel Settings

The **More Settings** section allows you to manage your WhatsApp Business Profile, configure conversational components, check your WhatsApp number's status, and access Marketing Messages Lite.

* Profile Manage - your WhatsApp Business Profile, business information, and QR codes.
* Conversation - Configure Ice Breakers and Commands for customer interactions.
* Status - View your WhatsApp number's connection status, quality rating, verification, and messaging capacity.
* Marketing Lite - Check eligibility and manage the Marketing Messages Lite feature.
