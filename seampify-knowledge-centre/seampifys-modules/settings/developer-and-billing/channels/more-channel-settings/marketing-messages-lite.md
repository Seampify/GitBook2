# Marketing Messages Lite

The **Marketing Messages Lite** section allows you to check whether your WhatsApp Business number is eligible for Meta's Marketing Messages Lite feature and view its activation status.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/mm lite (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Marketing Messages Lite (MM Lite)**, now referred to by Meta as the **Marketing Messages API for WhatsApp**, is a dedicated messaging API designed for sending WhatsApp marketing templates.

It provides an optimized delivery system for marketing messages and supports additional pricing and delivery optimization capabilities.

Depending on Meta's pricing configuration and eligibility requirements, it may help you optimize marketing message costs. However, lower costs are not guaranteed for every message.

{% hint style="info" %}
You can refer more from Meta [here](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/reference/whats-app-business-account?).
{% endhint %}

The Marketing Messages Lite panel displays the current status of your WhatsApp number.

## Check Eligibility

Click **Check eligibility** to check whether your WhatsApp number qualifies for the feature.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/mm lite (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once enabled, Seampify automatically routes marketing templates through the Marketing Messages endpoint without requiring you to change how you send messages.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** Marketing Messages Lite is subject to Meta's eligibility and onboarding requirements. Availability may vary depending on your WhatsApp Business Account configuration, location, and account status.
{% endhint %}
