# Quick Reply

The **Quick Replies** feature allows you to create and save pre-built messages that can be quickly sent to customers through your Seampify Inbox.

You can create different message types, including text, images, buttons, lists, and interactive messages, to make your customer communication faster and more consistent.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/quick reply (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Creating a Quick Reply

### Step 1: Select Channel & Type

1. Navigate to **Quick Replies**.
2. Click **+ New Quick Reply**.
3. Select the communication channel.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/quick reply (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Choose the message type you want to create.
4. Click **Continue**.

### Step 2: Build Content

The **Build Content** section allows you to configure your quick reply information and message content.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/quick reply (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Message Formatting

The message editor provides formatting options, including:

* Bold
* Italic
* Strikethrough
* Code formatting
* Emojis
* Variables

You can click the **@** icon or type `@` to insert a variable into your message.

For example:

> Hi `{{full_name}}` 👋 Thank you for reaching out to us.How may we assist you today? Feel free to share your enquiry, and we'll be happy to help! 😊

The `{{full_name}}` variable can be used to personalize the message using the customer's name.

You can see how your message will appear to customers in the **Live Preview**.

### Step 3: Preview & Submit

The **Preview & Submit** section allows you to review your quick reply before publishing.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/quick reply (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once your configuration is complete:

1. Review the quick reply summary.
2. Check the Live Preview.
3. Ensure the message content is correct.
4. Click **Publish**.

Alternatively, click **Save Draft** if you want to complete the quick reply later.

## Managing Quick Replies

Once published, your quick reply will appear in the Quick Replies dashboard.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/quick reply (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Click the three-dot menu to access the following actions:

* Edit - Edit your existing quick reply.
* Send - Access the option to send the quick reply.
* Preview - Preview your quick reply.
* Clone as Draft - Create a draft copy of your existing quick reply.
* Delete - Delete your quick reply.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/quick reply (6).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Sending Quick Replies from Inbox

Once your quick reply is published, you can use it directly within your customer conversations.

### Step 1: Open a Customer Conversation

1. Navigate to **Inbox**.
2. Open the customer conversation.
3. Locate the message composer at the bottom of the conversation.
4. Click the **+** icon beside the message input field.
5. Select **Quick Replies** from the Quick Actions menu.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/quick reply (7).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Step 2: Select a Quick Reply

The **Quick Replies** window will display the available quick replies for your conversation.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/quick reply (8).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Select the quick reply you want to send.

### Step 3: Preview & Send Your Quick Reply

After selecting a quick reply, the right-hand panel will display its content.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/quick reply (9).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* If your quick reply contains variables, the **Fill Variables** section will appear.

For example:

| **Variable**    | **Example Value**         |
| --------------- | ------------------------- |
| `{{full_name}}` | Seampify Customer Support |

* Review or enter the required variable values before sending.
* Variables such as `{{full_name}}` are automatically captured by Seampify and will appear in the variable field. You can either edit the variable if needed or simply leave it as it is. Seampify will automatically populate the corresponding information when sending the message.
* Next, click **Send Reply**.
