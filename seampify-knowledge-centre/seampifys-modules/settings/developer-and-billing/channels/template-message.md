# Template Message

The **Template Messages** feature allows businesses to create WhatsApp message templates for customer communication. Users can create templates manually or use AI to generate message content based on their business requirements.

Now you can choose to create template messages either manually or use AI to create one.

## Creating a Template with AI

The **Create with AI** feature allows users to generate WhatsApp message templates by providing your business information, selecting a use case, and describing your messaging requirements.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Choose Create with AI

1. Click **Create with AI** at the top-right corner.
2. The **Create Template with AI** form will appear.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/template ai (6).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Enter Template Information

The **Create Template with AI** form requires users to provide information about the message you want to generate.

<div><figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/template ai (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/template ai (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Generate Templates

After clicking **Generate Templates**, the AI will begin creating WhatsApp message template variations based on the provided information.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/template ai(3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The AI generates variants designed with WhatsApp approval requirements in mind. Final approval remains subject to Meta's review.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Review AI-Generated Templates

Once the generation process is completed, you can review the suggested template variations.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/template ai(4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

In the example shown, AI generated **four template variants**.

You can see the preview on how the final template should be.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Customize the Selected Template

After selecting an AI-generated template, users can review and customize its contents in the template editor.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/template ai(5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Preview and Submit

Once the template configuration is completed:

1. Review the message content and formatting.
2. Ensure all required media and sample values have been provided.
3. Check the WhatsApp Preview.
4. Review the Template Checklist.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/template ai (7).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Click **Preview & Submit** to proceed.

You can also click **Save Draft** to save their progress without submitting the template.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important:** AI-generated templates are not automatically approved by Meta. All submitted templates remain subject to Meta's WhatsApp message template review and approval process.
{% endhint %}
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## What's New✨ – Meta Approval Confidence

The **Meta Approval Confidence** feature help you evaluate whether your message template is likely to meet Meta's approval requirements before submission.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-11-23 171226.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The feature provides:

* **Approval Confidence Score:** Displays an estimated confidence percentage for template approval.
* **Confidence Level:** Indicates the estimated approval likelihood, such as Very High.
* **Category Validation:** Helps identify whether message content is suitable for its selected category, such as Utility or Marketing.
* **Policy Warnings:** Highlights potential content issues before submission.
* **Submission Readiness:** Indicates whether the template is ready to be submitted.

If the system detects content that may not comply with the selected template category, it will display a warning.

> 1 Error, must fix before submitting

Draft can be saved with these issues, and still be submitted to Meta.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important: Meta Has the Final Decision**

The Meta Approval Confidence feature provides an estimated assessment of whether a message template is suitable for approval under the selected category, such as **Utility** or **Marketing**.

However, a high confidence score does not guarantee approval. **Meta makes the final decision on template approval and category classification.** Meta may reject a template or assign a different category based on its review.
{% endhint %}
