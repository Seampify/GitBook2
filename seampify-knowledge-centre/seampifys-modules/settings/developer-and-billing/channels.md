# Channels

The **Channels** section allows businesses to connect and manage their messaging platforms within Seampify, including WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook Messenger.

Users can monitor channel connection statuses, access channel-specific settings, and view messaging activities.

## Accessing Channels

1. Log in to your Seampify account.
2. Navigate to **Settings → Channels**.
3. The Channels page will display all connected messaging accounts.
