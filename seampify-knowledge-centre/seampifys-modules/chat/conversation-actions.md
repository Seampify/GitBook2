# Conversation Actions

Under the **Overview** tab, agents can manage conversation ownership and collaboration.

### Assigned Agent

Assign the conversation to a specific agent.

Agents can also click **Assign to me** to assign the conversation to themselves.

### Assigned Team

Assign the conversation to the relevant department for further handling.

For example, a customer enquiry may be assigned to the Sales, Customer Support, or Technical Support department.

### Additional Members

Additional team members can be added to a conversation to support collaboration.

Click **+ Add member** to add another member to the conversation.

### Calendar Events

View calendar events associated with the selected customer.

Click **+ New** to create a new calendar event, such as a customer appointment, meeting, or scheduled follow-up.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
Click on the Tags field bar, type the desired tag, and hit Enter to create it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Click Save once you're done.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Refresh the page to have the newly added Tag appear in the drop-down list for future usage.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
