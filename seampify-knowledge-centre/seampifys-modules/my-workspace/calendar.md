# Calendar

The **Calendar** section allows users to manage scheduled events, customer appointments, meetings, and booking availability directly within Seampify through Google Calendar integration.

## Connecting Google Calendar

Before using the Calendar feature, users are required to connect their Google Calendar account and grant the necessary permissions.

### Connecting Your Account

1. Navigate to **My Workspace → Calendar**.
2. Click **Connect Google Calendar** at the top of the page.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/calendar (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Select the Google account you wish to connect.
2. Review the requested permissions.
3. Grant the required permissions to Seampify.
4. Complete the authorization process.

### Required Permissions

During the authorization process, Google will request permission for Seampify to access your Google Calendar.

This will allow Seampify to:

* View and edit events on all your calendars.
* See, edit, share, and permanently delete all the calendars you can access using Google Calendar.

> **Important:** Review the requested permissions before granting access. These permissions provide Seampify with access to the calendars available through your connected Google account.

Once the connection is successfully established, you can proceed with managing your calendar activities within Seampify.

## My Slots

The **My Slots** feature allows users to manage their availability for appointments and bookings.

This feature is useful for organizing available appointment times based on your working schedule and business requirements.

To access this feature:

1. Navigate to **My Workspace → Calendar**.
2. Click **My Slots**.
3. Configure your availability using the available settings.

## Public Booking

The **Public Booking** feature provides a convenient way for customers to access your booking availability.

This feature can be used for activities such as customer consultations, sales appointments, product demonstrations, and service bookings.

To access this feature, navigate to **My Workspace → Calendar → Public booking**.

## Creating a New Event

To manually schedule a calendar event:

1. Navigate to **My Workspace → Calendar**.
2. Click **+ New Event** at the top-right corner.
3. Complete the **New Event** form.
4. Click **Create Event** to save the event.
