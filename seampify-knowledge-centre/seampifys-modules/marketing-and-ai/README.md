# Marketing & AI

The **Marketing & AI** module in Seampify allows businesses to automate customer interactions, manage messaging campaigns, and leverage artificial intelligence to improve customer engagement and operational efficiency.

The module consists of three main sections:

| **Section** | **Description**                                                                        |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Automations | Create automated workflows to streamline customer interactions and business processes. |
| Campaigns   | Create, schedule, and monitor messaging campaigns.                                     |
| AI Agents   | Create and train AI agents to respond to customer enquiries automatically.             |

## Accessing Marketing & AI

1. Log in to your Seampify account.
2. Click **Marketing & AI** on the left-hand navigation menu.
3. Select **Automations**, **Campaigns**, or **AI Agents**.
