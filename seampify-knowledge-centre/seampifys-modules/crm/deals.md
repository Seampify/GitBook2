# Deals

The **Deals** section allows businesses to manage qualified sales opportunities, track potential revenue, and monitor sales progress until closure.

Unlike leads, deals generally represent more established sales opportunities that are progressing towards a purchase or business agreement.

## Deals Overview

Navigate to **CRM → Deals** to access your deal management dashboard.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Deals (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Deals page provides the following features:

| Feature      | Description                                                   |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Kanban       | Displays deals according to their current deal stages.        |
| Table        | Displays deals in a structured table format.                  |
| Date Range   | Filter deals based on the selected time period.               |
| All Channels | Filter deals based on their associated communication channel. |
| Search Deals | Search for deals using their title.                           |
| New Deal     | Create a new sales deal.                                      |

## Deal Pipeline

The Deal Pipeline provides a visual representation of sales opportunities as they progress through your organization's sales process.

In the Kanban view, deals are organized into columns based on the **Deal Pipeline Stages** configured by your organization.

Each stage displays the number of associated deals and their monetary value, allowing businesses to monitor sales progress and potential revenue.

### **Deal Pipeline Stages**

Deal stages are customizable based on your organization's sales workflow.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Deals (3).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Illustration of a deal pipeline in Kanban view.</p></figcaption></figure>

Below is an example of how deal stages can be configured:

| Example Stage | Description                                                                  |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Contacted     | Initial communication has been established with the potential customer.      |
| Meeting Set   | A meeting or product demonstration has been scheduled.                       |
| Presented     | A product, service, or proposed solution has been presented to the customer. |
| Quoted        | A quotation or pricing proposal has been provided to the customer.           |

**Note:** The stages above are for illustration purposes only. Businesses can configure their own deal stages according to their sales processes.

### Customizing Deal Stages

To configure your Deal Pipeline Stages:

1. Navigate to **Settings → CRM**.
2. Locate the **Deal Pipeline Stages** section.
3. Enter a new deal stage name.
4. Enter the stage percentage.
5. Click **+ Add Stage**.
6. Customize the stage color and arrangement as required.
7. Click **Save CRM Settings** to apply your changes.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Leads and Deals creation.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The configured stages will be reflected in your Deals pipeline.
