# Leads

The **Leads** section allows businesses to track potential sales opportunities and monitor customer interest throughout the sales pipeline.

A lead can represent a customer who has expressed interest in your products or services but has not yet become a confirmed sales opportunity.

## Leads Overview

Navigate to **CRM → Leads** to access your lead management dashboard.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Leads (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Leads page provides two viewing options:

| View   | Description                                                                  |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Kanban | Displays leads as individual cards organized according to their lead stages. |
| Table  | Displays leads in a structured table format.                                 |

Use the **Search leads by title** search bar to locate a specific lead.

## Lead Pipeline

The Lead Pipeline allows businesses to track potential sales opportunities throughout their sales journey.

In the Kanban view, leads are organized into columns based on the **Lead Lifecycle Stages** configured by your organization.

Each stage displays the number of associated leads and their monetary value, providing a visual overview of your sales opportunities.

### Lead Lifecycle Stages

Lead stages are customizable based on your organization's sales process and business requirements.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Leads (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Below is an example of how lead stages can be configured:

| **Example Stage** | **Description**                                                                                         |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cold Lead         | A potential customer who has shown initial interest but requires further engagement.                    |
| Warm Lead         | A potential customer who has demonstrated interest and may require additional information or follow-up. |
| Hot Lead          | A potential customer who has demonstrated strong buying interest and may be ready to proceed.           |

**Note:** The stages above are for illustration purposes only. Your organization's lead stages may differ depending on your CRM configuration.

### Customizing Lead Stages

To configure your Lead Lifecycle Stages:

1. Navigate to **Settings → CRM**.
2. Locate the **Lead Lifecycle Stages** section.
3. Enter a new lead stage name.
4. Enter the stage percentage.
5. Click **+ Add Stage**.
6. Customize the stage color and arrangement as required.
7. Click **Save CRM Settings** to apply your changes.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Leads and Deals creation.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The configured stages will be reflected in your Leads pipeline.

## Creating a New Lead

To create a new lead:

1. Navigate to **CRM → Leads**.
2. Click **+ New Lead** at the top-right corner.
3. Complete the **Create Lead** form.
4. Click **Create Lead** to save the information.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Leads (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Lead Creation Fields

| **Field**           | **Required** | **Description**                                             |
| ------------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Title               | Yes          | Enter a title for the lead.                                 |
| Channel             | Yes          | Select the communication channel associated with the lead.  |
| Contact             | Yes          | Select an existing contact or add a new contact.            |
| Lead Stage          | Yes          | Select the current stage of the lead.                       |
| Source              | Yes          | Select the source from which the lead originated.           |
| Deal Value          | No           | Enter the estimated monetary value of the opportunity.      |
| Expected Close Date | Yes          | Select the expected date for closing the sales opportunity. |
| Description         | No           | Enter additional notes, context, or next steps.             |

### Selecting a Contact

A communication channel must be selected before selecting a contact.

Once a channel is selected, the contact list will be filtered to display contacts reachable through that channel.

If the customer does not exist in the contact database, click **+ Add Contact** to create a new contact.

### Lead Assignment

Newly created leads are automatically assigned to the user who created them. Managers can subsequently reassign the lead to a team.
